Type: 		Half-Life Skin
Name: 		T.K. (Major K skin)
Release Date: 	27/04/99

Author:		Seven^3		mvincent@southpoly.ac.nz

Files:		TK.mdl, TK.bmp, and TK.txt

Instructions:	Create a folder <Half-life dir.>\valve\models\player\TK and unzip the
		files into there.

Info:		I was just playing around with Mdlviewer 2.1, checking out what various models					
		looked like with chrome skins, and seeing if I could get Major K's hair to hue					
		shift so I could have an avatar that looks a bit different. The orange/black							
		chromed hair looked cool, so I did the body in a similar fashion and now she
		looks like someone from "The Lawnmower Man".
		If you know how to add hue shifting to chrome textures please let me know.
		Once I get that sussed, I'll do a better one with my own face texture.

Tools used:	
	   Texture transfers:	Mdlviewer 2.1
	   Editing:		MS Paint and Photoshop 5.0
	   Viewing:		Mdlviewer 2.1 and Half-Life (-dev mode.)
